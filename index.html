The week has six days, not seven

一週實際只有六天，不是七天

The clock starts when last Wednesday's class ends and stops at 23:59 on Tuesday. Wednesday itself is class day and does not count. Budget roughly 40–50 minutes of individual reading and filling, 40 minutes of group discussion, and 20 minutes of consolidation — which is the two hours of outside-class work the syllabus asks for.

時間從上週三下課後開始算，到週二 23:59 截止；週三當天要上課，不計入。時間預算大致是：個人閱讀與填寫 40–50 分鐘、小組討論 40 分鐘、彙整 20 分鐘——這就是課綱要求的每週課外兩小時。

Your answers live in this browser only

你的答案只存在這台瀏覽器裡

Nothing is uploaded automatically. Export before you close the tab.
系統不會自動上傳。關掉分頁前請先匯出。

The worksheet saves what you type into this browser's local storage. That means it survives a refresh — but it does not follow you to another computer, and it disappears if you clear browsing data or work in a private window. Treat the export button as the save button.

學習單會把你填的內容存進這台瀏覽器的本機儲存空間。重新整理不會不見——但它不會跟著你換到另一台電腦，清除瀏覽資料或用無痕視窗也會消失。請把「匯出」當成「存檔」。

Two exports, two different jobs

兩種匯出，兩種用途

Both live in the last module, Completion & Export. Filenames are generated for you in ASCII with a timestamp to the second, so resubmitting never overwrites an earlier version — you get version history for free.

兩個都在最後一個模組「完成度與匯出」裡。檔名由系統自動產生，一律 ASCII 並附到秒的時間戳，所以重交不會覆蓋前一版——版本紀錄是免費附送的。

What completion percentage is, and is not

完成度是什麼、不是什麼

The worksheet shows a completion percentage. It is a threshold, not a score: below 80% counts as not submitted. Filling every box with something is not the goal — the three checkpoints in sections 5, 6 and 7 are judged on substance, and the wording of each checkpoint is shown to you inside the worksheet before you write. You are never marked against a standard you were not told.

學習單會顯示完成度百分比。它是門檻，不是分數：未達 80% 視同未繳。把每一格塞滿字不是目的——第 5、6、7 節的三個檢查點看的是內容，而每個檢查點的判準就寫在學習單裡、你動筆前就看得到。不會有沒被告知的標準拿來評你。

If you get stuck

卡住的時候

Every worksheet opens with module 0, Executive Summary, which says what that week is about and which material to read first. If a link will not open or an export will not download, tell the TA on Moodle — do not spend the evening fighting it.

每份學習單的第 0 模組「執行概要」會說明當週主題與必讀第一項。若連結打不開或匯出下載不了，直接在 Moodle 告訴助教——不要花一整晚跟它耗。
