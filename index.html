Weekly Worksheets

每週學習單

Introduction to Project Management and Career Strategy as a Product Manager in the AI Era — twelve worksheets across four three-week blocks. Each one is done individually first, then consolidated as a group.

AI 世代產品經理職涯策略與專案管理導論——四個三週區塊、共十二份學習單。每一份都先個人獨作，再由小組收攏成綜整成果。

12 worksheets份學習單 6 groups組 Prof. Shihmin Lo · IBS, NCNU駱世民 教授 · 暨大國企系
Submission. Every group submits the weekly worksheet on Moodle by 23:59 on the Tuesday before class, whether or not it is presenting that week. Sections 2 to 4 are individual work and are not submitted; sections 1, 5, 6 and 7 are the group's consolidated work. A worksheet revised after class in light of the discussion and resubmitted earns a Bonus Assignment point. 繳交方式。各組每週均須繳交，不論該週是否輪到簡報，於課前週二 23:59 前上傳 Moodle。第 2 至 4 節為個人獨作、不繳交；第 1、5、6、7 節為小組綜整成果。課後依課堂討論修訂並重新繳交者，計加分作業（BA）一次。
